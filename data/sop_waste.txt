MUNICIPAL SOP DOCUMENT: SOLID WASTE MANAGEMENT
Department of Sanitation
Document Reference: SAN-WASTE-SOP-2024

1. PURPOSE
This SOP governs response timelines and escalation for garbage collection,
overflowing bins, and illegal dumping complaints.

2. COMPLAINT RESPONSE TIMELINES
- Missed collection (single occurrence): Rectify within 24 hours by
  dispatching the nearest available collection vehicle.
- Overflowing community bin: Rectify within 12 hours; if a bin overflows
  more than twice in a month at the same location, its collection
  frequency must be permanently increased from alternate-day to daily.
- Illegal dumping (construction debris, bulk waste): Requires a
  dedicated debris-clearing team, response within 48 hours, and a fine
  notice if the dumping party can be identified via municipal cameras
  or resident testimony.

3. RECURRING NON-COLLECTION HOTSPOT PROTOCOL
If a street or locality generates 5 or more missed-collection complaints
within a 60-day window, this indicates a systemic route-planning failure
rather than a one-off lapse. Such cases must be escalated to the
Sanitation Route Planning Committee for route redesign, not merely
repeated ad-hoc dispatches.

4. HEALTH AND HYGIENE ESCALATION
Complaints mentioning "foul smell," "health hazard," or proximity to a
school/hospital must be treated as high priority regardless of whether
the 5-complaint recurring threshold has been met, and addressed within
12 hours.

5. EQUITY CONSIDERATION
Wards with historically low complaint volume should not be assumed to
have fewer sanitation issues -- low complaint volume may reflect lower
digital literacy or grievance-portal awareness rather than lower actual
need. Sanitation supervisors should conduct periodic physical audits in
such wards independent of complaint volume.

6. REPORTING
Monthly sanitation performance reports must break down missed-collection
incidents by ward to identify systemic versus isolated failures.
