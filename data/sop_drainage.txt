MUNICIPAL SOP DOCUMENT: DRAINAGE & WATERLOGGING MANAGEMENT
Department of Public Works and Drainage
Document Reference: PWD-DRAIN-SOP-2024

1. PURPOSE
This document lays out the Standard Operating Procedure for identifying,
prioritizing, and resolving waterlogging and drainage complaints raised
by citizens through the municipal grievance portal.

2. CLASSIFICATION OF WATERLOGGING COMPLAINTS
Waterlogging complaints are classified into three severity tiers:
- Tier 1 (Critical): Water entering residential premises, or waterlogging
  persisting for more than 72 hours after rainfall stops. Requires
  emergency pumping within 24 hours and a site inspection by the Ward
  Engineer within 48 hours.
- Tier 2 (Moderate): Road-level waterlogging affecting vehicular movement
  but not entering homes. Requires drain desilting within 7 days.
- Tier 3 (Minor): Temporary puddling that clears within a few hours of
  rain stopping. Logged for seasonal drain-cleaning schedule, no
  emergency action required.

3. RECURRING HOTSPOT PROTOCOL
If a single location (identified by ward and landmark) generates 5 or
more waterlogging complaints within a single monsoon season, it must be
escalated to the Chief Engineer's office as a "Recurring Drainage
Hotspot." Recurring hotspots require:
- A root-cause survey (checking for undersized culverts, illegal
  encroachment on drains, or silt accumulation).
- Inclusion in the following year's capital works budget for permanent
  remediation (culvert widening, new stormwater drain construction).
- A public notice board at the site indicating remediation timeline.

4. MOSQUITO BREEDING RISK
Any waterlogging complaint text mentioning stagnant water for more than
5 days must be simultaneously forwarded to the Public Health Department
for larvicide spraying, regardless of drainage severity tier.

5. RESOURCE ALLOCATION PRIORITY
Wards with three or more recurring hotspots in the same season should
receive priority allocation of desilting machinery and manpower over
wards with isolated, non-recurring complaints. This ensures equitable
but impact-weighted resource distribution.

6. CITIZEN COMMUNICATION
For every Tier 1 or recurring hotspot complaint, the ward office must
send an SMS/notification to the complainant within 48 hours acknowledging
receipt and providing an expected resolution timeline.
